Vectors

  • pCS2+hSpCas9 Map (PDF) · Sequence (GenBank) · Addgene #51815

    Human codon-optimized SpCas9 in pCS2+ for Cas9 mRNA synthesis (SP6). Works efficiently in fertilized eggs of aquatic animals, mainly teleosts such as medaka, zebrafish and various aquaculture species, and has also been used in cyclostome and amphibian embryos.
    Original paper: Ansai & Kinoshita, 2014

  • pUC-BaitD-Xhbb-EGFP Sequence (GenBank) · GitHub: Plasmids

    Donor vector for NHEJ-based Cas9 knock-in. It carries a minimal promoter from the Xenopus beta-globin gene, which keeps background expression low.
    Original paper: Kayo et al., 2023

  • Tip100 transposon vectors Sequences and distribution (Hoshino lab, NIBB)

    Vectors for highly efficient medaka transgenesis with Tip100, a DNA transposon from the common morning glory. The terminal sequences can be shortened to 100 bp on each end without losing transposition activity, and the germline transmission rate reaches 62.7%, which is exceptionally high among teleosts. Three plasmids are available: pCS2-Tip100 for transposase mRNA synthesis, pBS-Tip100-LR100-actb4k-EGFP (medaka actb 4-kb promoter) for assessing somatic integration, and pBS-Tip100-LR100-olvas-EGFP (olvas promoter) for germline integration and stable line establishment. They are distributed by NBRP Morning Glory under an MTA with a distribution fee.
    Original paper: Tanaka et al., 2025
